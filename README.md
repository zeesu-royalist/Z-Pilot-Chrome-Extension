## ✨ What is Z-Pilot?

Instead of opening 10 different tabs or installing 8 separate extensions, **Z-Pilot** gives you a single, beautiful side panel inside your browser.

* 🎯 **All-in-One:** AI Chat, Video Downloader, Ad Blocker, Notes, Tasks, Translator & Calculator in one place.
* ⚡ **100% Free Forever:** Powered by Google's official Gemini AI with no monthly subscription.
* 🔒 **100% Private:** Runs directly inside your browser. No middleman servers, no tracking, and no data collection.

*(If you are using **Brave**, use `brave://extensions` • If using **Edge**, use `edge://extensions`)*

### Step 3: Load the Extension
1. Click the button on the top-left: **"Load unpacked"**.
2. Select the folder you unzipped in Step 1.
3. Click the puzzle icon (🧩) in your browser toolbar and click the **Pin (📌)** icon next to **Z-Pilot**.

🎉 **You are done! Click the Z-Pilot icon in your toolbar to open your side panel.**

## 🔑 How to Get Your Free AI Key

Z-Pilot uses Google Gemini AI, which is **completely free** to use:

1. Go to **[aistudio.google.com/app/apikey](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey)** and sign in with your Google / Gmail account.
2. Click the blue **"Create API Key"** button *(No credit card required)*.
3. Copy your key.
4. Open Z-Pilot, paste the key in the settings box, and click **"Connect & Start"**.

## ❓ FAQ

**1. Is Z-Pilot completely free?**  
Yes! Z-Pilot is 100% free and open source. Google provides a generous free daily quota for Gemini AI that easily covers everyday use.

**2. Will it slow down my browser?**  
No. Z-Pilot is very lightweight. In fact, its built-in ad blocker stops heavy ads and trackers from loading, making web pages open faster.

**3. Does Z-Pilot store or track my data?**  
No. There are no remote servers. All your notes, tasks, and settings stay safely on your computer inside your browser.

**4. Which browsers are supported?**  
Google Chrome, Brave, Microsoft Edge, Opera, Vivaldi, and all other Chromium browsers.
